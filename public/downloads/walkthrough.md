0:00 - 0:15 | Introduce the leadership story.
"Building a more inclusive Nigeria begins with people already at work. President Bola Ahmed Tinubu appointed Ayuba Gufwan Executive Secretary of NCPWD on 6 August 2024. This independent demonstration presents his public engagements alongside contributions from representatives, institutions and partners."

0:15 - 0:50 | Move through all eight chapters.
"Follow mobility and participation in Otukpo; farming support and livelihoods; education advocacy at UBEC; civic rights and election monitoring; regional access in Abia; enforcement training; partner-supported health outreach; and stronger institutions through CBM, NOA and disability-data coordination. Each chapter links its report and identifies leadership, partners and the limits of the evidence."
Scroll through the chapters in this order: mobility, livelihoods, education, civic rights, communities, enforcement, health, institutions. Open a chapter photograph to show the original image proportions, caption, date, location, source and photo credit. The Abia photograph depicts the partner engagement and does not show Gufwan. A representative-led event photograph does not establish Gufwan's personal attendance.

0:50 - 1:10 | Use the compact timeline and explain the evidence trail.
Select a year or date marker and use Previous event / Next event to inspect one event and its caveat. The appointment is initially selected. The opening photographs advance continuously every four seconds. Arrows, indicators and swipe select a photograph; hover, focus and reduced-motion settings currently do not pause this slideshow.
"These reports distinguish advocacy, monitoring, delivery and proposed cooperation. Election barriers remain visible. Agriculture has a date conflict; the Abia office and NDMIS deployment remain unverified. Enforcement and health work explicitly credit Gufwan's representatives and contributing partners."
Presenter notes: UBEC, 26 September 2024, establishes advocacy. Election monitoring, 21 September 2024, records an access barrier in Edo and violence in Kwara. The 14 June 2025 enforcement report credits Gufwan with the keynote and explicitly names Ikem Ochigbelam as his representative. Lawrence Idemudia represented Gufwan at the 18 December health outreach, supported by TLM, CHAI, CBM and Sightsavers. The 31 October 2024 CBM visit proposed deeper cooperation.
