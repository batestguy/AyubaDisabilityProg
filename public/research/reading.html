4. Building on your work: one database, an adaptive cycle

  • Your community relationships, outreach and practical support
  • Proposed extension: Designed together
  • Adaptive sampling: Use recorded recruitment evidence to adjust the next outreach round, investigate gaps and decide where to focus effort.
  • Understand needs: Review recorded skills, interests and access needs with participants to understand the learning and support that would suit them.
  • Match teaching: Use recorded interests and learning needs to identify suitable teaching. The learner and facilitator agree the arrangement.
  • Support practice: Use the agreed learning plan and support needs to guide accessible teaching, practical tasks and feedback.
  • Monitor & review: Record learning progress and recruitment evidence in the database. Use progress to adjust support and recruitment evidence to refine outreach.
  • Participant database: Keep recruitment details, skills, learning needs, agreed support and progress together. These records inform decisions throughout the cycle.
  • Adaptive sampling: Recruitment feeds the database; its records guide the next round.
  • Adaptive sampling: Adaptive recruitment feeds the participant database. Recorded recruitment evidence guides each new sampling round.
  • Understand needs: Skills, interests and learning needs
  • Match teaching: Agree suitable teaching with the learner and facilitator
  • Support practice: Accessible teaching and practical feedback
  • Monitor & review: Monitor recruitment evidence and learning progress, update the participant database, then use the updated records to refine recruitment, teaching and support. The cycle repeats.
  • Participant database: Recruitment routes; Skills and interests; Learning needs; Agreed teaching and support; Learning progress
  • Recruitment evidence → outreach: Recruitment evidence informs each new outreach round; the cycle repeats.
  • Learning progress → support: Learning progress informs teaching and support adjustments.
  • Proposed Plateau pilot · Records inform decisions throughout the cycle
