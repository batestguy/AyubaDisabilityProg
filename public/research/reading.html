3. Talent is everywhere. Access to learning should be too.

  • Through Beautiful Gate, your work has helped remove mobility barriers for people like James Ahumbe.
  • We propose to build on that foundation through adaptive sampling and a participant register capturing people’s skills, interests and learning needs.
  • This would help us connect participants with suitable teaching, monitor their learning progress and adjust support as their needs change.

Historical Beautiful Gate beneficiary, identified by name and photograph on page 3 of Wheelchairs for Nigeria’s July 2023 newsletter. The source identifies James Ahumbe as a cobbler who received a replacement tricycle. The photograph shows him with that tricycle; it does not show shoemaking or establish that Beautiful Gate taught him his trade. The proposed learning pathway and sandbox are separate prospective work; no partnership, endorsement or learning outcome is implied.
