## Clearer, more visual slide 5 — 11 November 2026

Owner asked what adaptive snowball sampling actually does and asked for a clearer slide 5 with more visuals. Slide 6 approved unchanged.

What the research supports (basis for the wording):
- Snowball recruitment: people already reached invite others they know. Suited where no list of the population exists and trust matters (Sadler et al., 2010, *Nursing & Health Sciences*, <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3222300/>; Johnson, 2014).
- Known weakness: starting contacts shape who is found; well-connected people and people similar to the starters are over-represented; isolated people are missed; results are not representative (Magnani et al., 2005, *AIDS*, <https://doi.org/10.1097/01.aids.0000172879.20628.e1>; Johnson, 2014; Heckathorn, 1997).
- "Adaptive" means changing course between rounds from what the records show: new and different starting contacts, outreach to under-reached groups, other routes (Sadler et al., 2010; Swedish disability precedent already linked on the slide).
- We claim neither representativeness nor respondent-driven sampling. Both points are in the slide note.

Changes (shared source `src/researchSlides.ts` → `recruitmentContent`; render branch in `scripts/build-research-presentation.mjs`; styles in `public/research/deck.css`):
- Four text-only vertical stages replaced by **three rounds of dots** (native inline SVG, same gold/cream palette): Round 1 varied trusted contacts; Round 2 referrals spread, one group stays hollow and is marked "Not reached yet"; Round 3 a new starting contact inside that group lights it up. A bracket returns from Round 3 to Round 1 labelled "Next round". A key explains the three dot types (filled gold, filled cream, hollow, so meaning does not rely on colour alone).
- Each drawing is a labelled image whose text alternative gives that round's detail; the group has a text alternative listing all three rounds.
- New answer line: "Each round shows who our networks reach — so we can go looking for who they miss." Distinction now visible: "Referrals make it snowball. Checking and changing course makes it adaptive." Visible limit: "People with few connections may need other routes."
